# E0: Meta runtime smoke gate

## Goal

Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor (`muse-spark-1.3-contributor`), through Meta Model
API, must complete a scripted five-call MCP sequence in at least 9 of 10 fresh
trials from the development environment in India. Record every trial's
pass/fail result, latency, token usage, and exact error text. Results from
OpenCode Go, the Standard-mode `muse-spark-1.3`, or any other provider are not
E0 results.
