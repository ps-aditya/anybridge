# Hackathon facts

Checked 2026-11-01 against the official event page:
https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon

## Confirmed on that page

- Event: Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, hosted by Meta and Cerebral Valley.
- Hackathon begins Oct 26, 2026; submissions are due Nov 4 at 9:00 PM ET.
- Judging window listed as Nov 4–8.
- There are Indie developer and Startup tracks.
- Participants may use Muse models through Meta Model API, Meta open-weight
  models, and Muse Code.
